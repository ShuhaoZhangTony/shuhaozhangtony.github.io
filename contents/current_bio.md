# 当前个人简介

张书豪，华中科技大学计算机科学与技术学院教授，主持科技部重大项目课题，入选国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外），获批国家自然科学基金外国优秀青年学者研究基金项目（RFIS-II）。研究以面向国产算力的极致性能推理引擎为系统内核，面向智能体应用构建持续数据与推理系统。以 vLLM-HUST 为工程载体，研究推理调度、KV 状态管理、执行与通信优化，并围绕内核扩展数据流编排、外部记忆、动态数据维护与评测能力。长期从事并行与分布式系统和数据流处理研究。加入华中科技大学前，曾任新加坡南洋理工大学助理教授，并在德国柏林工业大学从事博士后研究。

## Short Bio

Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). In 2026, he was awarded support through the NSFC Research Fund for International Excellent Young Scientists (RFIS-II). His research aims to build high-performance inference engines for domestic accelerators in China, forming the core of continuous data and inference systems for agent applications. With vLLM-HUST as an engineering vehicle, he studies inference scheduling, KV-state management, execution and communication optimization, and extends this core with orchestration, external memory, dynamic data maintenance, and evaluation components. His broader research spans parallel and distributed systems and stream processing. Before joining HUST, he was an Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University and a postdoctoral researcher at Technische Universität Berlin.
