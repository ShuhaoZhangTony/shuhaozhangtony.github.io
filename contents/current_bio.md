# 当前个人简介

张书豪，华中科技大学计算机科学与技术学院教授，主持科技部重大项目课题，入选国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外），获批国家自然科学基金外国优秀青年学者研究基金项目（RFIS-II）。主要研究动态环境下的数据密集型系统，围绕依赖驱动的任务执行、持续变化信息的维护与复用、资源约束下的在线服务调控开展工作。当前结合大模型推理、动态检索与有状态数据处理开展研究，并以 vLLM-HUST 等开源系统连接机制研究、工程实现与真实负载验证。加入华中科技大学前，曾任新加坡南洋理工大学、新加坡科技设计大学助理教授，并在德国柏林工业大学从事博士后研究。

## Short Bio

Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). In 2026, he was awarded support through the NSFC Research Fund for International Excellent Young Scientists (RFIS-II).

His research focuses on data-intensive systems in dynamic environments. His group studies dependency-driven task execution, the maintenance and reuse of continuously changing information, and online service control under resource constraints. Current work spans LLM inference, dynamic retrieval, and stateful data processing, with open systems such as vLLM-HUST connecting research mechanisms to engineering and evaluation on real workloads. Before joining HUST, he was an Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University and the Singapore University of Technology and Design, and a postdoctoral researcher at Technische Universität Berlin.
