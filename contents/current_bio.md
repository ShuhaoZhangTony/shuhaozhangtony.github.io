# 当前个人简介

张书豪，华中科技大学计算机科学与技术学院教授，主持科技部重大项目课题，入选国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外），获批国家自然科学基金外国优秀青年学者研究基金项目（RFIS-II）。主要研究并行与分布式系统中的高效执行和状态管理，包括硬件感知执行、共享状态并发控制、在线状态迁移与故障恢复。当前重点面向国产算力上的推理服务，以 vLLM-HUST 为工程载体，研究请求调度、KV 状态管理与高效执行，同时开展推理工作流、外部记忆和动态检索研究。加入华中科技大学前，曾任新加坡南洋理工大学、新加坡科技设计大学助理教授，并在德国柏林工业大学从事博士后研究。

## Short Bio

Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). In 2026, he was awarded support through the NSFC Research Fund for International Excellent Young Scientists (RFIS-II).

His research focuses on efficient execution and state management in parallel and distributed systems, including hardware-conscious execution, concurrency control over shared state, online state migration, and failure recovery. His current systems agenda centers on inference serving for domestic accelerators in China. Using vLLM-HUST as a research and engineering platform, he studies request scheduling, KV-state management, and efficient execution, alongside work on reasoning workflows, external memory, and dynamic retrieval. Before joining HUST, he was an Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University and the Singapore University of Technology and Design, and a postdoctoral researcher at Technische Universität Berlin.
