# 当前个人简介

张书豪，华中科技大学计算机科学与技术学院教授，主持科技部重大项目课题，入选国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外）。长期从事并行与分布式系统、数据流处理系统和大模型推理系统研究，近年来聚焦状态管理驱动的推理系统优化，重点关注状态感知调度、硬件感知记忆管理、KV/中间状态复用以及推理服务稳定性等问题。加入华中科技大学前，曾任新加坡南洋理工大学助理教授，并在德国柏林工业大学从事博士后研究。当前工作围绕大模型推理引擎、推理服务系统与记忆智能体中间件展开，探索共享状态在观测、管理与执行三个层面的系统化组织，以及相关机制对吞吐、P99 时延和服务稳定性的影响。

## Short Bio

Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). His research focuses on state-management-driven optimization for inference systems, spanning parallel and distributed systems, LLM inference infrastructure, state-aware scheduling, hardware-aware memory management, state reuse, and serving stability. Before joining HUST, he was an Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University and a postdoctoral researcher at Technische Universität Berlin.
