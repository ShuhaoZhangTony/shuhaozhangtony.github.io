\cvsection{Profile}
Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). In 2026, he was awarded support through the NSFC Research Fund for International Excellent Young Scientists (RFIS-II).

His research focuses on efficient execution and state management in parallel and distributed systems: hardware-conscious execution, shared-state concurrency control, online migration, and recovery. His current systems agenda centers on inference serving for domestic accelerators in China, using vLLM-HUST to study request scheduling, KV-state management, and efficient execution. Related work explores reasoning workflows, external memory, and dynamic retrieval.

\cvsection{Representative Systems}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{vLLM-HUST / BidKV}: Inference serving on domestic accelerators and utility-guided preemption under KV pressure.
  \item \textbf{SAGE}: Dataflow-native execution for modular and controllable LLM reasoning.
  \item \textbf{Neuromem}: External-memory lifecycle decomposition and evaluation.
  \item \textbf{BriskSeed / FlowRAG / StreamFP / GRACE / CANDOR-Bench}: Online search-history reuse, dynamic retrieval, stream learning, graph maintenance, and continuous-ANNS evaluation.
  \item \textbf{MorphStream / Spacker / CStream}: Dependency-driven scheduling, distributed state migration, and hardware-conscious stream processing.
\end{itemize}
