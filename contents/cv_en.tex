\cvsection{Profile}
Shuhao Zhang is a Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He leads a task under a major national science and technology project of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China and is a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). In 2026, he was awarded support through the NSFC Research Fund for International Excellent Young Scientists (RFIS-II).

His research aims to build high-performance inference engines for domestic accelerators in China, forming the core of continuous data and inference systems for agent applications. With vLLM-HUST as an engineering vehicle, he studies scheduling, KV-state management, execution and communication optimization. Orchestration, external memory, dynamic data maintenance, and evaluation components extend this core toward long-running agents.

\cvsection{Representative Systems}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{vLLM-HUST}: vLLM-based research and engineering stack for inference on domestic accelerators; the core of the systems agenda.
  \item \textbf{SAGE}: Dataflow orchestration extending the inference core toward modular, controllable LLM reasoning.
  \item \textbf{Neuromem}: External-memory lifecycle research and benchmarks; part of the continuous-state extensions.
  \item \textbf{BriskSeed / FlowRAG / StreamFP / GRACE / CANDOR-Bench}: Online search-history reuse, dynamic retrieval, stream learning, graph maintenance, and continuous-ANNS evaluation.
  \item \textbf{MorphStream / TStream / CStream}: Stream processing systems for multicore, stateful, and hardware-conscious execution.
\end{itemize}
